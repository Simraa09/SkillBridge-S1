# SKILLBRIDGE

### Personalized Career Skill Assessment and Preparation Platform

**SkillBridge** is a web-based platform designed to help students and freshers assess their technical knowledge, improve their skills, and evaluate their readiness in selected technical domains.

The platform provides a structured learning and assessment journey through baseline assessments, dynamically generated practice tests, performance tracking, and reassessments.

It aims to help learners understand their current level of preparation and work towards achieving their desired readiness.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Provide students with a platform to assess their technical knowledge.
- Evaluate their existing understanding through baseline assessments.
- Generate practice assessments based on the selected technical domain.
- Gradually increase question difficulty as students progress.
- Track assessment scores and learning progress.
- Evaluate improvement through periodic reassessments.
- Help students measure their readiness against a predefined score threshold.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **Student Authentication:** Secure student registration and login.
- **Domain Selection:** Choose between Java and SQL.
- **Baseline Assessment:** Evaluate existing knowledge through an initial MCQ-based assessment.
- **Dynamic Practice Assessments:** Generate practice tests using questions from the selected domain's question bank.
- **Progress Tracking:** Maintain assessment history, scores, and performance progress.
- **Reassessment:** Allow students to evaluate their readiness after practising.
- **Readiness Evaluation:** Compare assessment performance against the configured readiness threshold.
- **Certification Resources:** Provide relevant external Oracle certification information after achieving the required readiness level.
- **Admin Management:** Manage domains, questions, and readiness thresholds through the Django Admin interface.

---

## 🔄 How SkillBridge Works
The platform follows a structured student journey:

1. **Register / Login** – Students create an account or log in using their credentials.
2. **Select Domain** – Choose a technical domain: Java or SQL.
3. **Baseline Assessment** – Attempt an initial assessment to evaluate existing knowledge.
4. **View Results** – Review the assessment score and readiness status.
5. **Practice** – If the required readiness threshold is not achieved, students continue with dynamically generated practice assessments.
6. **Track Progress** – View scores and assessment progress.
7. **Reassessment** – Attempt a reassessment to evaluate improvement.
8. **Readiness Completion** – Once the required threshold is achieved, students receive a completion message and relevant Oracle certification resources.

The practice and reassessment cycle continues until the student achieves the configured readiness threshold.
